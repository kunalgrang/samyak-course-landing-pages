Samyak Skill Circle

Learn, Refer and Grow

A referral programme for current Samyak students, former students and alumni who want to refer friends, relatives or immediate family members to Samyak Computer Classes.

Ask for Referral Details View Full Rules

Programme Highlights

  • Open to current students, former students and alumni.
  • No maximum limit on genuine referrals.
  • Accepted referrals remain valid for 90 days.
  • Rewards are approved after admission and required fee verification.

How It Works

A simple referral process with clear rules

Referrers use their unique personal referral link, or register a referral physically at the Samyak centre. The first valid referral recorded in the system receives priority.

1. Share your personal link

The prospective student submits the referral form through your unique link. Your code is recorded automatically and is not visible inside the form.

2. Referral is checked

Samyak validates the mobile number, referral timing, existing enquiry records and referral eligibility.

3. Reward is approved

The reward becomes eligible after admission, required fee receipt, payment verification and owner approval.

Final Referral Programme Rules

Samyak Skill Circle Rules

These rules define who can participate, how referrals are recorded, when rewards apply, and how privacy and disputes are handled.

1. Who can participate

The referral programme is open to current Samyak students, former Samyak students and alumni.

  • There is no maximum limit on genuine referrals.
  • Students and alumni may refer friends, relatives and immediate family members.
2. How referrals must be registered

Every referrer will receive a unique personal referral link. The referrer must share this link with the interested person.

The prospective student must submit the referral form containing:

  • Full name
  • Mobile number
  • Email address, optional
  • Course interested in
  • Contact-consent confirmation

The referrer's unique code will be automatically recorded through the personal link and will not be visible inside the form.

A referral may also be registered physically at the Samyak centre. The counsellor must enter it into the referral system at the time of the visit.

3. First valid referral rule

When the same prospective student is referred by more than one person, the first valid referral recorded in the system will receive the reward.

  • The decision will be based on the system-generated date and time of submission.
  • The prospective student's normalised 10-digit mobile number will be used as the primary identifier.
  • Samyak will not disclose the identity of another referrer.
4. Invalid referrals

A referral will not be accepted when the prospective student:

  • Is already registered as an enquiry with Samyak
  • Is currently studying at Samyak
  • Has previously studied at Samyak
  • Was already referred through an earlier active referral
  • Provides an incorrect or invalid mobile number
  • Is submitted without a valid personal referral link or verified physical referrer
5. Referral validity

Every accepted referral will remain valid for 90 days from the date of submission.

If the prospective student does not take admission within 90 days, the referral will expire. After the original referral expires, the person may be referred again through a new valid referral. The first new referral submitted after expiry will receive priority.

6. Successful referral definition

A referral becomes eligible for a reward when:

  1. The referred student completes the admission process.
  2. Samyak receives at least 50% of the student's final agreed course fee.
  3. The payment is verified by the counsellor or owner.
  4. The admission has not been cancelled.
  5. The owner approves the referral reward.

The 50% requirement will be calculated on the final course fee after all approved discounts, not on the standard advertised fee.

Example: Standard course fee: ₹40,000. Final fee after discount: ₹35,000. Minimum payment required: ₹17,500. The referral becomes reward-eligible after Samyak receives at least ₹17,500.

The reward will be determined according to the referred student's final course fee after discounts.

8. Cash reward rules
  • Cash rewards will be paid through UPI or another method approved by Samyak.
  • Payment details will be requested only after the reward becomes eligible.
  • The payment date and transaction reference will be recorded internally.
  • The referrer will receive a WhatsApp confirmation after the reward is paid.
9. Samyak course-credit rules
  • Course credit may be used for another Samyak course.
  • Multiple referral credits may be combined.
  • Credit may be used by the referrer or transferred to an immediate family member.
  • Immediate family includes parents, children, siblings and spouse.
  • Samyak may request reasonable proof of the relationship.
  • Course credit cannot be exchanged for cash.
  • Course credit will be valid for 12 months from the approval date.
  • The credit will be adjusted against the final agreed fee after applicable discounts.
  • Any unused amount will remain available until the credit expiry date.
10. Admission cancellation and reward recovery

If the referred student cancels the admission or receives a qualifying fee refund after a referral reward has been approved or paid:

Unpaid reward

The pending reward will be cancelled.

Paid cash reward

Samyak may request repayment from the referrer, or adjust the amount against the referrer's future rewards.

Used or issued course credit

Samyak may cancel the unused credit, or adjust the value against future referral rewards. The adjustment and its reason will be recorded in the referral system.

11. Benefit for the referred student

Every successfully admitted referred student will receive a complimentary AI Tools Crash Course.

  • Conducted as a classroom session
  • No separate cash value
  • Non-transferable
  • Available only to the admitted referred student
  • To be attended within three months of admission
  • Scheduled according to available Samyak batches

Suggested topics include using ChatGPT effectively, prompt-writing basics, AI for study and research, AI for office productivity, AI for resumes and job preparation, and responsible use of AI tools.

13. Privacy

The referrer will not receive the referred student's fee amount, payment details, counselling discussions, personal academic information, reasons for admission rejection, or any other confidential information.

The referrer will only receive referral-related status updates.

14. Dispute resolution

Referral disputes will be resolved using:

  • Referral submission date and time
  • Normalised mobile number
  • Existing enquiry and student records
  • Physical referral records
  • Referral activity logs

The earliest valid system record will be treated as final. Samyak may reject referrals involving fraud, false information, manipulated submissions or misuse of another person's referral link.

Important Note
